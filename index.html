Our Story

Born from Passion & Purpose

Trading Times was founded with a single mission: to make the global forex market accessible to everyday Nigerians. Starting from a small WhatsApp group in Port Harcourt, we have grown into Nigeria’s most trusted forex training institution.

With over 4,200 students trained across all 36 states, we’ve proven that with the right knowledge and community, anyone can build consistent income from the markets.

Practical-First LearningEvery lesson links directly to real market application — not just theory.
